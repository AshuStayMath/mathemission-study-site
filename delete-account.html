Delete your account and data

For the apps Mathemission Study (study.mathemission.in) and Mathemission Teach (teach.mathemission.in), published on Google Play by Mathemission (AK, Ashutosh Kumar).

How to ask for deletion
  1. Send an email to mathactiveonly@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Mathemission account". Say whether you use Study, Teach or both.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

We only act on requests sent from the account's own email address, so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in email) and profile.
  • Your study data: tests and answers, scores, time per question, mistake notebook, revision schedule, progress and settings.
  • Your notes and note links, handwriting (rough work) and any photos you uploaded.
  • Your Ask AK messages and replies.
  • Teach: your lecture notes, question sets and device sign-ins.
  • Records of explanation clips you played.

What we keep

  • Records of purchases made on mathemission.in (order number, email, product, amount), because tax law requires us to keep them, normally for up to 8 years. They are not used for anything else.

Deleting only some data

You can delete your own notes, handwriting, photos and Ask AK messages yourself inside the app at any time. If you don't sign in, your progress stays only on your device: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it. To have only some data deleted from your account, email us as above and say what to remove.
